Privacy Policy — AI Workout

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Workout (listed on the App Store as “AI Workout Coach & Gym Tracker”, “the App”, “we”, “us”), developed by Boris Serzhanovich, handles your information. The App is a fitness and workout tracking application for iOS and watchOS. By using the App you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

CategoryExamplesPurpose
Health & Fitness Heart rate, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep (via HealthKit); workouts, sets, reps, weights, and body measurements you enter To provide core app functionality: tracking, statistics, and recovery insights
Name The display name you provide during onboarding (and optional profile info if you sign in with Apple or Google) To personalize the app experience and link cloud community safety features
Photos Progress/“Before & After” photos and Smart Capture images To let you track visual progress. Photos are stored locally on your device

2. HealthKit Data

When you grant permission, the App reads health metrics from Apple HealthKit solely to display them inside the App and compute fitness insights. We do not use HealthKit data for advertising or marketing, and we do not share it with third parties. HealthKit data is processed on your device.

3. How Your Data Is Stored

Most of your data — workouts, measurements, photos, and settings — is created and used on your device. Depending on the feature, data is stored as follows:

  • iCloud sync (Apple CloudKit) — If you are signed in to iCloud, your fitness data (workouts, sets, reps, weights, body measurements, and weight history) is synced across your devices using your private iCloud account (CloudKit private database, container iCloud.com.borisdev.WorkoutTracker). This data is stored in your personal iCloud space, is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy, and is not accessible to us. Health-derived metrics read from HealthKit are not written to CloudKit beyond the workout/measurement values described above.
  • Shared workouts you publish are stored in our cloud database (Google Firebase Firestore).
  • Safety features (blocked-user lists and content reports) are stored in Firestore to keep the shared community safe.
  • To enable the community features above, the App creates an anonymous account (Firebase Anonymous Authentication). No email or password is required.

4. AI Features

When you use AI Coach or AI generation features (after accepting the in-app AI consent notice), the prompts and non-sensitive training context you submit (body weight and Apple Health data are never sent) are transmitted over HTTPS to our backend (Google Cloud Functions) and routed via OpenRouter to third-party AI language models to generate a response. When you publish a shared workout, its title, description, and exercise notes are also screened by automated AI moderation via OpenRouter. Requests are protected by Firebase App Check and per-account rate limits. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information in AI prompts.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, App Check, Remote Config, Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring) — Firebase Privacy
  • OpenRouter (AI model routing & automated workout moderation) — OpenRouter Privacy
  • Apple HealthKit & iCloud (CloudKit) — governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy
  • Sign in with Apple / Google Sign-In — optional authentication if you choose to link an account

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for tracking across apps or websites owned by other companies. Content you explicitly choose to share (e.g., shared workouts) becomes visible to other users of the App.

7. Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time from Settings → Delete Profile & Data (local data) and Settings → Account → Delete Account, which permanently removes your account and all associated server-side data (shared workouts, blocked list, and reports). This action cannot be undone.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can exercise deletion directly in the App, or contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the latest revision.
